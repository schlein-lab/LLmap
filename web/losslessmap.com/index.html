About LLmap

LLmap is the open-source mapper with native wave-particle adaptive λ, GPU + AI optional, and per-region Bayesian priors. It targets long-read mapping in high-noise, high-SV, and otherwise difficult regions where minimap2's seed-chain-extend pipeline degrades (metagenomes, structural variants, repetitive plant/rodent genomes).

Source: github.com/schlein-lab/LLmap. Methodology: methodology.html.
